How it works

01 / 03

Play something.

Pick a built-in track, hold your mic up to the room, or drop in an audio file. One tap and it starts.

02 / 03

It listens.

Several times a second, the sound is split into 16 bands from bass to treble. This happens in your browser. No audio leaves it.

03 / 03

It grows.

Each moment becomes a slice and time runs up the form. Change track and the colour shifts. Drift off and that height keeps a notch.

    Maker note

    Why this exists.

    I think it's cool to watch music turn into art. Afterglow takes whatever I'm listening to while I study and builds a sculpture out of it, one slice at a time.

    The catch is that the piece only finishes if I do. Stop early and it stays half grown. Wanting to see the whole thing is what keeps me at my desk.
